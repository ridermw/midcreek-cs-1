How this build is proved

This page only ships after a headless browser gate loads the same WebAssembly package, waits for the game to report data-game-state="ready", proves the canvas renders approved palette colour, and confirms no error or unhandled rejection was captured.

Click the canvas to focus it. While it is focused the arrow keys, Q, E, and Space drive the game instead of scrolling this page.

Move the technician with the arrow keys, orbit the camera with Q and E, and repair the highest priority faulted rack with Space while standing next to it.

The build is generated from repository-owned sources only: the glTF assets are regenerated and byte-compared by the asset gate, and the browser shell has no package manager, bundler, or third-party script.
